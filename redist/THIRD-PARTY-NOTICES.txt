TaskbarHWM - third-party components
===================================

TaskbarHWM talks to the PawnIO kernel driver to read CPU temperature and power.
PawnIO is by namazso (https://pawnio.eu).

Files distributed with TaskbarHWM
---------------------------------

PawnIOLib.dll  (version 2.2.0, unmodified, digitally signed by namazso)
    The user-mode helper library for the PawnIO driver.
    License: GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later (see COPYING.LGPL-2.1.txt).
    Source:  https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO  (directory PawnIOLib)
    TaskbarHWM loads this DLL dynamically; you may replace it with another build of PawnIOLib
    by dropping it next to TaskbarHWM.exe.

AMDFamily17 and IntelMSR modules  (release 0.2.11, unmodified, signed; embedded in TaskbarHWM.exe)
    Small PawnIO modules that read CPU sensors.
    License: GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later (see COPYING.LGPL-2.1.txt).
    Source:  https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO.Modules

Not distributed, installed separately
-------------------------------------

PawnIO driver (PawnIO_setup.exe 2.2.0)
    Installed by the TaskbarHWM setup wizard (or from the app's Settings) only when it is not
    already present. It is downloaded from the official release page
    https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO.Setup/releases/tag/2.2.0 and checked against a pinned
    SHA-256 before it is run. The driver is licensed under GPL-2.0-or-later with an exception for
    programs that communicate with it through its device interface. Uninstalling TaskbarHWM does
    not remove PawnIO, because other programs may use it.

NVIDIA NVML (nvml.dll)
    Loaded from the NVIDIA display driver already installed on your PC. Not distributed with
    TaskbarHWM.
